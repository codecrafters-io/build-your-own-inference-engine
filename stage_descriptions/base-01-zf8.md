GPT-2, an early model from the makers of ChatGPT, predicts the next word of a text by doing arithmetic with 124 million numbers, called its weights. It learned those numbers during [training](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/mini-llm), by reading a huge amount of text and adjusting them until its guesses got good. In this stage, you'll implement the `inspect` command to print a summary of the file that holds them.
