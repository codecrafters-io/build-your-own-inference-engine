GPT-2, an early model from the makers of ChatGPT, predicts the next word of a text by doing arithmetic with 124 million numbers, called its [weights](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#weight). It learned those numbers during [training](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#training), by reading a huge amount of text and adjusting them until its guesses got good. In this stage, you'll implement the `inspect` command to print a summary of what's inside a weights file.
