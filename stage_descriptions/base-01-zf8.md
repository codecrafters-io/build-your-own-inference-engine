GPT-2, an early model from the makers of ChatGPT, predicts the next word of a text by doing arithmetic with 124 million numbers, called its [weights](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#weight). It learned those numbers during [training](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/glossary#training), by reading a huge amount of text and adjusting them until its guesses got good. The program that loads those weights and does the arithmetic is called an inference engine, and it's what you'll build in this challenge. In this stage, you'll implement the `inspect` command to print a summary of what's inside a weights file.
